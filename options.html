Muslim Reminder Settings

About

Muslim Reminder shows dhikr reminders and prayer times on your new tab page.

Version 1.0.0

Dhikr Reminders

Dhikr reminders are shown each time you open a new tab. A new dhikr is displayed randomly from the collection.

Prayer Times

Prayer times are configured from the new tab page. You can select your city or use GPS to detect your location automatically.

Shortcuts

Shortcuts are managed from the new tab page. Click the "+" button to add a new shortcut.

Reset Data

Clear all saved shortcuts and preferences.
